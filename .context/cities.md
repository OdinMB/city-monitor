# Cities: what each one has, what is missing, what is pending

_Last updated: 2026-11-03._ One overview per city: which sources it has, what was looked for and not found (or ruled out), and which requests to data owners are still unanswered. The details live in the topic files linked from `CLAUDE.md`; this file is the map. Update it in the same change whenever a city gains or loses a source, a request is answered, or a search comes up empty.

The research behind the Köln, München and Hamburg rows (licence verdicts, URLs, dead ends) is in the PM repo: `city-monitor-pm/references/engineering/2026-11-01_koeln-muenchen-hamburg-sources.md`. Feed licence verdicts are in [news.md](news.md) ("Feed status").

## Every city

Weather (BrightSky), DWD warnings, air quality (WAQI/Open-Meteo, plus the official state-network stations from the UBA Air Data API since 2026-11-03: 15 BLUME stations in Berlin, 14 Hamburger Luftmessnetz stations, 4 LANUK stations in Köln, 5 LfU stations in München; [air-quality-stations.md](air-quality-stations.md)), rain radar, traffic incidents (TomTom), NINA alerts, DWD pollen, pharmacies (aponet), defibrillators (OSM), roadworks ([roadworks.md](roadworks.md)), the labour-market tile (BA Eckwertetabelle: the Land table for Berlin and Hamburg, the Kreis table for Köln and München; [social-atlas.md](social-atlas.md)), the wastewater tile (RKI AMELAG plants, plus LAGeSo for Berlin's flu and RSV; [wastewater.md](wastewater.md)), the electricity tile (since 2026-11-03: SMARD generation mix of the city's control area, 50Hertz for Berlin and Hamburg, Amprion for Köln, TenneT for München; Germany's CO₂ intensity and grid signal from Energy-Charts, which has no regional values; no day-ahead price while SMARD's licence page puts wholesale prices on hold; [electricity.md](electricity.md)), the soil-moisture tile (since 2026-11-03: DWD daily soil moisture under grass at the city's climate stations, Berlin-Tempelhof, -Dahlem and -Buch, Hamburg-Fuhlsbüttel and -Neuwiedenthal, Köln/Bonn, München-Stadt; CC BY 4.0; [soil-moisture.md](soil-moisture.md)) and the crisis-line tile (hand-kept directory per city, re-check each November; [new-data-sources.md](new-data-sources.md)). Since 2026-11-03 also the bike-sharing map layer, one system per city ([bike-sharing.md](bike-sharing.md)): nextbike's GBFS feeds (CC0-1.0) for nextbike Berlin, KVB-Rad in Köln and MyRadl in München (its stations inside the city only), and StadtRAD from Hamburg's SensorThings API (dl-de/by-2.0). Not added: Call a Bike (DB Connect, via MobiData BW; the feed states no licence, so it waits for a confirmation) and Dott's public GBFS (no licence stated). Since 2026-11-03 also the traffic-accident map layer from the Unfallatlas (dl-de/by-2.0, newest year 2025: Berlin 13,280, Hamburg 7,057, Köln 4,922, München 5,264 injury accidents), filtered by municipality key ([traffic-accidents.md](traffic-accidents.md)). Since 2026-11-03 also the EV charging map layer from the BNetzA Ladesäulenregister (CC BY 4.0, monthly, filtered by Kreis; [ev-charging.md](ev-charging.md)); Hamburg adds the live status of each public charge point. No open live charging status was found for Berlin, Köln or München (Mobilithek has no open "Ladepunkte dynamisch" offer). Since 2026-11-03 also the heat-relief map layer, which the weather tile points to when DWD warns of heat or its heat trend expects it ([heat-relief.md](heat-relief.md)): drinking fountains in all four cities, cool places in Berlin and Köln, public toilets in Berlin, Köln and München.

Built but off (2026-11-03): lifts and escalators out of service at DB stations (S-Bahn and regional) from DB FaSta (CC BY 4.0), in the lift tile of every city. It needs a free DB API Marketplace key (`DB_API_CLIENT_ID`, `DB_API_KEY`) the owner has not registered yet; until then Berlin, Hamburg and München show no lift tile and Köln KVB only. The parser follows the OpenAPI spec and is untested against live data ([lift-outages.md](lift-outages.md#db-stations-fasta--built-not-yet-live)).

Since 2026-11-03: hospitals with their G-BA emergency tier from the Bundes-Klinik-Atlas open-data export, a sub-layer of the emergencies map layer in every city (2026-10-27 export: Berlin 57 sites, Hamburg 32, Köln 18, München 42). On by default; `KLINIK_ATLAS=false` on the API service hides it (Berlin, Köln and München then show no hospitals, Hamburg its own WFS). Open request: the export states no licence or reuse terms, and the owner is asking the G-BA whether it may be reused here and with what credit; no licence is claimed until they answer, and the owner sets `KLINIK_ATLAS=false` if they object ([hospitals.md](hospitals.md#the-klinik-atlas-opt-out)).

## Berlin

- **Has:** everything but citizen reports (below). On top of the shared sources: 6 news feeds with AI briefing, police (berlin.de), VBB transit, events (kulturdaten.berlin, Ticketmaster, go~mus), Spree/Havel/Dahme gauges and bathing water, water temperature at ten Wasserportal stations (rivers, canals, lakes; dl-de/by-2.0; the water layer's temperature sub-layer, [water-levels.md](water-levels.md#water-temperature-wasserportal-berlin)), council meetings (BVV OParl + Abgeordnetenhaus), political data (Bundestag, Abgeordnetenhaus, district mayors; roll-call votes for the Bundestag only until the Abgeordnetenhaus 2026–2031 has some on abgeordnetenwatch), the Abgeordnetenhaus election of 2026-09-20 by Wahlkreis (Amt für Statistik, CC BY 3.0 DE, election view of the political layer since 2026-11-03; [election-results.md](election-results.md)), budget, social atlas, population, LAGeSo wastewater (flu and RSV; SARS-CoV-2 from AMELAG like the others) with the RKI's notified flu and RSV cases for the Land beside it (IfSG, CC BY 4.0, since 2026-11-03; [wastewater.md](wastewater.md#reported-cases-rki-ifsg)), fire brigade, Bürgeramt appointments (Firecrawl), live noise sensors, noise map, rent map, heat relief (cool rooms, drinking fountains and toilets from GDI Berlin, all dl-de/zero-2.0), and since 2026-11-03 Gieß den Kiez street-tree waterings (CC0) in the soil-moisture tile ([soil-moisture.md](soil-moisture.md)), street trees (street and park trees from the Baumbestand WMS, dl-de/zero-2.0, since 2026-11-03; [street-trees.md](street-trees.md)). Since 2026-11-03 also street counts from citizen-run Telraam sensors (Berlin zählt Mobilität, CC BY 4.0; 88 Berlin streets counted on 2026-11-02), a sub-layer of the traffic layer ([telraam-counts.md](telraam-counts.md)). The crime map from the Kriminalitätsatlas (Polizei Berlin, CC BY-SA, 143 Bezirksregionen, PKS 2025; since 2026-11-03, [crime-atlas.md](crime-atlas.md)).
- **Stale data to check:** the hand-kept district-mayor table (verified 2026-11-01) has only Reinickendorf's and Spandau's mayors elected for the new term; the other ten BVVs had not yet elected their Bezirksamt, so it lists the outgoing mayors. Re-check in December 2026. The pinned editions (MSS 2025, Wohnlagenkarte 2026, population 2026h01, noise maps 2022, Doppelhaushalt 2026/27) are in the freshness inventory in [new-data-sources.md](new-data-sources.md).
- **Ruled out:** BZ Berlin (AI-input ban), taz Berlin (terms forbid automated access; removed 2026-11-01).
- **Not yet available:** citizen reports. Ordnungsamt-Online (LABO, dl-de/by-2.0) lists an open-data endpoint, but on 2026-11-03 it closed the connection without a reply; re-test before building ([citizen-reports.md](citizen-reports.md)). The reports layer is Köln and München only until then.
- **Pending requests:** none.

## Hamburg

- **Has:** 3 news feeds (NDR, MOPO, Feuerwehr Hamburg) with AI briefing, police (Polizei Hamburg on presseportal), Elbe gauges, bathing water (17 spots, Geodienste `HH_WFS_Badegewaesser`, dl-de/by-2.0; [water-levels.md](water-levels.md)), roadworks (Bauweiser OGC API), council meetings (Bürgerschaft committees and plenary, from its website calendar), political data (Bundestag, Bürgerschaft), the Bürgerschaft election of 2025-03-02 by Stadtteil (Statistikamt Nord, election view of the political layer since 2026-11-03; the file is not catalogued with a licence, so its credit rests on Statistikamt Nord's imprint, an open question for the owner; [election-results.md](election-results.md)), noise map, rent map (Wohnlagenverzeichnis, `HH_WMS_Wohnlagen`, dl-de/by-2.0, normal and good locations), crisis tile, street trees (Straßenbaumkataster WMS, dl-de/by-2.0, since 2026-11-03; street trees only, no height; [street-trees.md](street-trees.md)). Labour market (BA Land table `Bundesland=Hamburg`) and wastewater (AMELAG plants `Hamburg 01` and `Hamburg 02`, combined per week weighted by the 0.58 and 1.46 million people they serve), since 2026-11-02; the wastewater tile also shows the RKI's notified flu and RSV cases for the Land (IfSG, CC BY 4.0), since 2026-11-03. Social atlas (Sozialmonitoring Bericht 2025, 857 Statistische Gebiete: unemployment, children of single parents, SGB II/AsylbLG recipients, children on minimum income, with Hamburg's own Statusindex; shares back-calculated from the report's z-scores; [social-atlas.md](social-atlas.md)) and population (Stadtteil statistics, 99 Stadtteile, yearly; [population.md](population.md)), since 2026-11-02. Transit: HVV announcements from the Geofox GTI, built 2026-11-02 after Hochbahn granted access (the request of 2026-11-01 is answered); it runs only where `GEOFOX_USER` and `GEOFOX_PASSWORD` are set, so production shows it once the owner sets them on Render, and a fork needs its own ([transit.md](transit.md#hamburg)). Since 2026-11-03 rail and ferry alerts also go on the map, placed through GTI's station list; bus-only alerts stay in the tile. Since 2026-11-03 the traffic layer has three more sub-layers ([traffic-counts.md](traffic-counts.md)): the live Verkehrslage congestion map (WMS `wms_hh_verkehrslage`, every 5 min, from zoom 12) and the hourly car and bicycle counters of the Urban Data Platform's SensorThings API (~170 car and ~80 bicycle sites, last hour against the median of the same hour over four weeks), all dl-de/by-2.0. Live status of Hamburger Energiewerke Mobil's ~2,540 public charge points (Urban Data Platform SensorThings, dl-de/by-2.0) on the EV charging layer, since 2026-11-03 ([ev-charging.md](ev-charging.md)). Hospitals (37, WFS `HH_WFS_Krankenhaeuser`, Behörde für Gesundheit und Verbraucherschutz, dl-de/by-2.0) as a sub-layer of the emergencies layer, with whether each takes part in emergency care, since 2026-11-03; merged with the Klinik-Atlas tiers unless `KLINIK_ATLAS=false` ([hospitals.md](hospitals.md)). Parking map layer since 2026-11-03: 45 car parks with live free spaces from the Parkleitsystem WFS (`wfs_parkhaeuser`, dl-de/by-2.0; [parking.md](parking.md)).
- **Looked for, not found or ruled out:**
  - Traffic: the Verkehrslage is also a WFS with `zustandsklasse` per segment (vector lines, jam lengths) and has a 10-minute forecast ("Verkehrslageprognose", FROST server); the raster WMS was enough for the map, so neither is built. A "cycling today" tile from the counters' daily totals is not built either.
  - News: Hamburger Abendblatt (feed terms forbid passing items on; removed), hamburg.de (feed gone), WELT (AI-input ban), ZEIT, taz Nord, t-online (blanket automated-access bans).
  - Events: hamburg.events (formerly MRH events) needs a usage agreement.
  - Budget: Offener Haushalt still only covers the 2019–2022 plan.
  - Appointments: possible only through Firecrawl's paid plan.
  - Two Elbe gauges (Bunthaus, Seemannshöft) have no characteristic values, so they show their level without a state. St. Pauli is tidal and reads "very high" often.
  - Crisis tile: the child-protection line is labelled generically.
  - Bathing water: Hamburg publishes no colour rating, so quality is derived from the newest sample's remark and algae note; its EU classification codes (`eg_einstufung`) are undocumented and not shown. Samples stop in early September.
  - Council meetings: the Bürgerschaft has no OParl, RSS or iCal feed (only its website calendar, now wired); the Parlamentsdatenbank and Transparenzportal have no upcoming dates. The seven Bezirksversammlungen' ALLRIS (`sitzungsdienst-<bezirk>.hamburg.de`) is in no OParl list, and its hosts refused our test machine, so an OParl endpoint is unconfirmed either way; re-check from a German IP ([council-meetings.md](council-meetings.md)). Still refused (connection reset) on 2026-11-02.
  - Citizen reports: the Melde-Michel has no open API or dataset (only the portal and a 2017 contract on the Transparenzportal).
  - Crime per area: the PKS is published only as yearly PDFs on the Transparenzportal, and the Stadtteil statistics have no crime fields (2026-11-03).
  - Heat relief: only the drinking fountains (BUKEA WFS, dl-de/by-2.0, 10 fountains). No open list of cool places or public toilets found on the Transparenzportal (2026-11-03).
  - Political map, Bezirk view: Hamburg's seven Bezirke are led by Bezirksamtsleitungen, administrative heads, not elected mayors, and the Bezirksamt pages name them without a party (Eimsbüttel's checked 2026-11-02), so there is nothing official to colour the outlines by. The Bundestag and election views are shown.

## Köln

- **Has:** 4 news feeds (WDR Rheinland, Kölner Stadt-Anzeiger, EXPRESS, Stadt Köln) with AI briefing, police (Polizei Köln on presseportal, also covers Leverkusen), Rhein gauge, roadworks (city WFS, permit periods), events (Stadt Köln open data), council meetings (SessionNet OParl 1.1), KVB lifts and escalators out of service (open data, dl-de/zero-2.0, own tile; [lift-outages.md](lift-outages.md)), citizen reports from "Sag's uns" (Open311, dl-de/zero-2.0, map layer, since 2026-11-03; [citizen-reports.md](citizen-reports.md)), political data (Bundestag members for Köln I–III and Leverkusen – Köln IV, Landtag NRW members for Köln I–VII, by candidacy; the Oberbürgermeister and 9 Bezirksbürgermeister from a hand-kept table), crisis tile, street trees (Baumkataster WMS, dl-de/zero-2.0, since 2026-11-03; [street-trees.md](street-trees.md)), labour market (BA Kreis table `Köln, Stadt`) and wastewater (AMELAG plant `Köln`), the last two since 2026-11-02. The 9 Stadtbezirk outlines (Offene Daten Köln, dl-de/zero-2.0) on the map, and a political map layer that colours them by their Bezirksbürgermeister's party. Social atlas and population from the Statistischer Datenkatalog (data year 2025, 570 Statistische Quartiere, since 2026-11-02): unemployment rate, single-parent households, SGB II recipients; residents, age and foreign share, yearly. Heat relief since 2026-11-03: cool places (three geoportal lists: indoor, air-conditioned, by the water), drinking fountains (geoportal) and public toilets (AWB), all dl-de/zero-2.0.
- **Looked for, not found or ruled out:**
  - Notified flu and RSV cases (RKI IfSG datasets): by Land only, no Kreis, so the wastewater tile shows none for Köln (checked 2026-11-03).
  - News: Kölnische Rundschau left out because it repeats the Stadt-Anzeiger's newsroom; no WDR feed for Köln alone.
  - Transit: KVB publishes lift and escalator outages only (shown in their own tile, which also lists KVB stations outside the city, such as Bensberg); line disruptions exist only as an unlicensed web page. gtfs.de carries no KVB alerts.
  - Bathing water: NRW publishes only the yearly EU classification of its bathing waters, no live samples, so not built.
  - Rent map: no open Wohnlagen dataset found.
  - Noise map: NRW's statewide strategic noise map (LANUV, 2022 round, dl-de/by-2.0) is on the map since 2026-11-02, road, rail and air with no combined layer; it draws only from zoom 13 ([noise.md](noise.md)).
  - Social atlas: the Datenkatalog has no child-poverty figure (sub-layer hidden) and no status index.
  - DWD has no UV forecast for Köln.
  - Notified flu and RSV cases (RKI IfSG datasets): by Land only, no Kreis, so the wastewater tile shows none for Köln (checked 2026-11-03).
  - Crime per area: offenedaten-koeln.de has PKS CSVs for 2007–2016 only (2026-11-03).
  - Political map: only the Bezirksbürgermeister view. Its Bundestag and Landtag constituencies (Köln I …) are not Stadtbezirke, so they stay in the tile.
  - Election results: the Kommunalwahl of 2025-09-14 could not be read. `wahlen.stadt-koeln.de` reset every connection from our test machine on 2026-11-03, and Offene Daten Köln has no election results after 2009. Re-test from a German IP; the job takes Köln as a new provider ([election-results.md](election-results.md)).

## München

- **Has:** 3 news feeds (BR24 Oberbayern, Rathaus Umschau since 2026-11-04 with the press office's permission, headline and teaser only; Feuerwehr München) with AI briefing, roadworks (Geoportal WFS, filtered), council meetings (RIS RSS feeds, about 10 days ahead), Isar flood class and flood warnings for the München and Puppling gauges (LHP, [water-levels.md](water-levels.md)), citizen reports from "Mach München besser" (Open311, CC0, map layer, since 2026-11-03; [citizen-reports.md](citizen-reports.md)), political data (Bundestag members for München-Nord/-Ost/-Süd/-West-Mitte, Bayerischer Landtag members for Stimmkreise 101–109, by candidacy; München-Land left out; the Oberbürgermeister from a hand-kept table), crisis tile, labour market (BA Kreis table `München, Landeshauptstadt`, not the wider Agentur für Arbeit München) and wastewater (AMELAG plant `München`, serving about 1.24 million people), the last two since 2026-11-02. The 25 Stadtbezirk outlines (GeodatenService München, dl-de/by-2.0) on the map. Social atlas and population from the Indikatorenatlas (Statistisches Amt, dl-de/by-2.0, one CKAN dataset per indicator, since 2026-11-02), at the 25 Stadtbezirke only, which the sidebar says ("25 districts only"): unemployed share of 15–64-year-olds, single-parent households, employable SGB II recipients and children under 15 on SGB II, data year 2024, no status index; residents, age and non-German share for the newest year (2025), yearly ([social-atlas.md](social-atlas.md), [population.md](population.md)). Heat relief since 2026-11-03: drinking fountains (Baureferat) and public toilets (GeodatenService), both dl-de/by-2.0 and credited on the Sources page.
- **Looked for, not found or ruled out:**
  - Notified flu and RSV cases (RKI IfSG datasets): by Land only, no Kreis, so the wastewater tile shows none for München (checked 2026-11-03).
  - Heat relief: no open list of cool places (opendata.muenchen.de, 2026-11-03).
  - News: Süddeutsche and tz forbid automated access; Abendzeitung unclear. (Rathaus Umschau, private use only under muenchen.de's terms, was allowed by the press office on 2026-11-04.)
  - Police: Polizeipräsidium München is not on presseportal, has no feed, and its robots.txt disallows all crawling.
  - Transit: MVG's messages API is unofficial and tolerates only private use. gtfs.de's open real-time feed (CC BY-SA 4.0) carries MVG alerts; the owner chose to wait for MVG's answer rather than build on it.
  - Events: no open source (opendata.muenchen.de has only historic CSVs).
  - Crime per area: nothing on opendata.muenchen.de (2026-11-03).
  - Isar water levels in cm: the Isar is not on PEGELONLINE, and LHP gives flood classes only. Levels exist only on HND pages (private use) or GKD downloads (CC BY 4.0, no API); they need the LfU's permission or the HND API it has announced.
  - Political map: no representative view, though the outlines ship. Its constituencies are not Stadtbezirke and it has no Bezirk-level office holders to colour them by. The Stadtrat election of 2026-03-08 by Stadtbezirk is shown instead (Wahlamt, dl-de/by-2.0, since 2026-11-03; [election-results.md](election-results.md)).
  - The 25 Bezirksausschuss chairs: one web page each, no dataset; too many to keep by hand.
  - Bathing water: Bavaria publishes no live samples as open data, so not built.
  - Notified flu and RSV cases (RKI IfSG datasets): by Land only, no Kreis, so the wastewater tile shows none for München (checked 2026-11-03).
  - Rent map: no open Wohnlagen dataset found.
  - Noise map: the LfU's agglomeration noise map (CC BY 4.0) covers München, but on 2026-11-02 its server returned empty tiles for about half of identical requests, so not built; re-test ([noise.md](noise.md)).
  - Street trees: no tree register on opendata.muenchen.de (a search for "Baumkataster" returned nothing on 2026-11-03), so no trees layer ([street-trees.md](street-trees.md)).
  - Social atlas and population at a finer level: the Indikatorenatlas keeps only the 25 Stadtbezirke current (coarse next to the other cities' 99–857 areas); "Bevölkerung Stadtbezirksteile" (108 areas) stops at December 2017. No composite status index.
